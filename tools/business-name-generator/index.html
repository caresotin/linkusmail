Naming tips

Prefer a .com under 12 characters. Avoid numbers, hyphens and hard-to-spell words. Check trademark conflicts before committing. And when you check availability, also look at the domain's history — a domain with a spammy past is a fresh start you don't want.

Honest caveat: generated names may already be trademarked or registered — always verify legally before use. Domain availability links open the registrar's search; they don't reserve the name.
