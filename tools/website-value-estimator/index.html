How the estimate works

The math is intentionally simple and fully visible:

  • Monthly revenue = visitors × pageviews/visitor × revenue per 1,000 pageviews × niche multiplier ÷ 1,000
  • Annual profit = monthly revenue × 12 × profit margin
  • Estimated value = annual profit × valuation multiple

Where backlinks fit in

Valuations assume the site keeps its rankings. Rankings depend on authority — and authority, for most sites, depends on the quality of the backlink profile. A site whose links are thin or spammy gets discounted by buyers (if they notice) and by Google (they always notice). Editorial links from real publishers are the asset that protects and grows the value this calculator estimates.

Honest caveats: this is a planning estimate, not an appraisal. Real valuations weigh traffic quality, revenue concentration, SEO risk and dozens of other factors. Use it to compare sites quickly — then verify with data. For your own site, the free audit shows the authority side of the equation.
