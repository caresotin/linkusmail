How the math works

This calculator uses a simple, transparent funnel — no black box:

  • Orders needed = target revenue ÷ average order value
  • Visitors needed = orders needed ÷ conversion rate
  • Traffic gap = visitors needed − current monthly organic visitors
  • Backlinks estimate = a planning range based on industry experience: roughly 1 editorial backlink per 150–300 monthly visitors of new search traffic, with diminishing returns as authority compounds

Why backlinks are in the plan

On-page SEO tells Google what your page is about. Backlinks tell Google whether anyone believes it — and for most competitive US keywords, they are the difference between page 3 and page 1. Editorial links from American publishers are the sustainable, white-hat way to build that authority, which is exactly what our service system delivers.

Honest caveats: conversion rates and backlink-to-traffic ratios vary wildly by niche. Treat the output as a planning range, not a promise — anyone who guarantees exact traffic is guessing. The free audit tightens these numbers for your specific site and market.
