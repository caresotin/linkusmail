Why pattern words work

Every day, Google handles searches no one has ever made before — and a large share of them follow the same patterns. “Generator”, “converter”, “checker” and similar words signal a user who wants to do something right now. That intent makes them valuable: the searcher is ready to use a tool, not just read about one.

How to use the ideas

  • Generate ideas, then verify volume and difficulty (use our KD checker)
  • One keyword = one page; build the page around the exact phrase
  • Check whether the top 10 are homepages of giants — if so, add a modifier (year, country, audience, format)
  • Pair each tool page with a few related “example” or “how to” pages to build internal links
Honest caveat: this tool generates patterns, not guaranteed demand. Real search volume can only come from keyword data (Google Keyword Planner, Semrush or similar) — use this to build the list, then verify before investing. Our free audit includes exactly this kind of verification for your market.
