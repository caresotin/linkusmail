How it works

The tool scans your text for standard email patterns, keeps only valid-looking addresses, removes duplicates and sorts them. It filters out common false positives like example.com placeholders.

Privacy & fairness: your text never leaves the browser. Use extracted addresses responsibly — respect privacy laws and only contact people who reasonably expect to hear from you.
