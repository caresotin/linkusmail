The two formulas

  • KGR = monthly searches ÷ allintitle results. Under 0.25 = strong opportunity; 0.25–1 = possible; over 1 = weak. Use with long-tail keywords under ~250 monthly searches.
  • KDROI = (volume × CPC) ÷ KD. Higher is better — it ranks keywords by value-per-difficulty. Sort a candidate list by KDROI and build pages from the top down.
Honest caveat: both formulas are heuristics, not guarantees. Always sanity-check the actual SERP (see our difficulty checker) before committing — and remember the page still needs to be genuinely better than what's ranking.
