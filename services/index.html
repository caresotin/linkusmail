The system

Five stages. Every campaign. Every time.

STAGE 01

Discover

We audit your site and backlink profile, map your target keywords, and build a shortlist of the right American publishers for your niche.

STAGE 02

Vet

Every site passes four checks: real US publisher, organic traffic, editorial standards, topic relevance. Link farms and PBNs never make the list.

STAGE 03

Pitch

People write the emails, not scripts. Each pitch is personal, each follow-up timed. We keep going until an editor answers.

STAGE 04

Place

Editorial links go live — in-content mentions, byline posts, or media coverage. Disclosed as sponsored where required. Always white-hat.

STAGE 05

Report

You get the URL, the publisher, the proof — plus what to expect next from Google. Every placement documented, every week accounted for.

Quality standards

The lines we never cross.

What we do

  • Real American publishers with organic traffic and an actual audience
  • Personal emails written by people, to real editors
  • Sponsored disclosure where the publisher requires it
  • Relevance first: your link earns its place in the content

What we never do

  • No link farms or directories built to sell links
  • No private blog networks (PBNs)
  • No mass-spam outreach or templated blasts
  • No ranking guarantees — anyone who offers them is lying
